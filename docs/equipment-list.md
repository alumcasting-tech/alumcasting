# Equipment List

## AlumCasting Production Equipment


AlumCasting operates an integrated die casting and machining facility in Dongguan, China, supporting aluminum and magnesium component production for automotive, EV, and industrial customers.


Website:

https://alumcasting.com


---


## Die Casting Equipment


### Aluminum High Pressure Die Casting (HPDC)

- Maximum machine capacity: 5000T
- Large tonnage aluminum die casting production
- Suitable for automotive and industrial components


### Magnesium Alloy Die Casting

- Maximum machine capacity: 3500T
- Magnesium alloy die casting production
- Lightweight structural component manufacturing


### Semi-Solid Die Casting (SSM)

- Maximum machine capacity: 2250T
- Semi-solid molding (SSM) technology
- High precision aluminum and magnesium components


---


## Machining Equipment


### CNC Machining

- Precision CNC machining after die casting
- Secondary machining for finished components
- Integrated casting and machining production line
