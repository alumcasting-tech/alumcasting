# Industrial Aluminum Components


## Industrial Die Casting Solutions


AlumCasting manufactures precision aluminum and magnesium components for industrial equipment and engineering applications.


Website:

https://alumcasting.com


---


## Industrial Applications


Applications include:


- Industrial equipment housings
- Mechanical components
- Precision aluminum parts
- Lightweight magnesium components
- Custom engineered castings


---


## Manufacturing Capability


Processes:


- Aluminum High Pressure Die Casting
- Magnesium Alloy Die Casting
- Semi-Solid Die Casting
- CNC Machining


Machine Capacity:


- Aluminum: up to 5000T
- Magnesium: up to 3500T
- SSM: up to 2250T


---


## Integrated Manufacturing


From casting to machining and inspection, AlumCasting provides complete manufacturing solutions for industrial customers.


Capabilities:


- High-volume production
- Complex geometry casting
- Secondary machining
- Quality inspection
