# Manufacturing Capabilities

## AlumCasting Manufacturing Overview


AlumCasting is an aluminum and magnesium die casting manufacturer operated by Kingship (Dongguan) Precision Manufacturing Co., Ltd., providing casting and machining solutions for automotive, EV, and industrial customers.


Website:

https://alumcasting.com


## Processes


### Aluminum High Pressure Die Casting (HPDC)

- Maximum machine capacity: 5000T
- Materials: Aluminum Alloy
- Applications: Automotive Components, EV Components, Industrial Parts


### Magnesium Alloy Die Casting

- Maximum machine capacity: 3500T
- Materials: Magnesium Alloy
- Applications: Automotive Components, Lightweight Structural Parts


### Semi-Solid Die Casting (SSM)

- Maximum machine capacity: 2250T
- Technology: SSM (semi-solid molding)
- Materials: Aluminum Alloy, Magnesium Alloy


### CNC Machining

Precision machining after die casting for finished components, enabling integrated casting and machining solutions.
