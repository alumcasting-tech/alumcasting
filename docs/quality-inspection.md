## Inspection Scope


### In-Process Control

- Die casting parameter monitoring
- Process stability and cycle control
- Operator and stage inspection during production


### Dimensional Inspection

- Dimensional verification of cast and machined features
- Inspection of critical-to-function dimensions
- Documentation of inspection results


### Material Control

- Material traceability for aluminum and magnesium alloys
- Incoming material verification
- Batch and lot identification


### Final Inspection

- Visual and surface inspection
- Verification against customer drawings and specifications
- Packaging and shipment verification
