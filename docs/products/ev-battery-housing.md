# EV Battery Housing


## Aluminum Die Casting Battery Housing Solutions


AlumCasting manufactures aluminum die casting battery housings and related structural components for electric vehicle battery systems.


Operated by:

Kingship (Dongguan) Precision Manufacturing Co., Ltd.


Website:

https://alumcasting.com


---


## Component Application


Product:

EV Battery Housing


Applications:

- Battery pack enclosures
- Battery module structures
- Thermal management components


---


## Manufacturing Process


Capabilities:


- Aluminum HPDC up to 5000T
- Semi-Solid Die Casting (SSM) up to 2250T
- CNC machining
- Dimensional inspection


---


## Material Solutions


Materials:


- Aluminum alloy


Benefits:


- Lightweight structure
- Dimensional stability for battery assembly
- Integrated casting and machining
