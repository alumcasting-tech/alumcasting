## Manufacturing Capabilities


AlumCasting supports automotive component production through:


- Aluminum High Pressure Die Casting (HPDC)
- Magnesium Alloy Die Casting
- Semi-Solid Die Casting (SSM)
- CNC Machining


Capacity:


- Aluminum HPDC: up to 5000T
- Magnesium Die Casting: up to 3500T
- SSM: up to 2250T
